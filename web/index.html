Why these filters

Every other events listing sorts by date and category. Neither one answers the question people actually have, which is "will I feel out of place if I walk in?"

So the level of observance is a 1–5 scale, and the things that actually decide comfort — kosher food, Shabbat observance, mixed or separate seating, whether there's a real service, whether a beginner will be looked after — are tracked separately, because they cut across the scale. Plenty of Reform events are strictly kosher; plenty of Orthodox events are mixed-gender socially.

When we can't tell, the event says so rather than guessing. A wrong religiosity label is worse than a missing one.
