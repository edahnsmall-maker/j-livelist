What happens to this, and why it's shaped this way

A model can read a flyer and pull out the title, date, time, place, and price quite reliably. What it cannot reliably infer is whether the food is kosher, whether there's a mechitza, whether phones are put away, or whether a newcomer will be looked after. Those aren't printed on most flyers — but the person running the event knows them instantly.

So the labor is split: the flyer carries what's easy to extract, and you supply the four things that are hard to infer and most decide whether someone feels at home walking in. That's why the form is short, and why "Not sure" is offered everywhere — a wrong label is worse than a missing one.

This proof of concept has no server, so it builds a submission you copy and send. In a deployed version this posts straight to the list and the flyer is read automatically.
