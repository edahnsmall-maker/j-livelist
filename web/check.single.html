Export disabled in your group? Paste messages instead

A group admin can switch on WhatsApp's Advanced Chat Privacy, which disables "Export chat" for everyone in the group. Copying the posts you actually care about still works. Paste them here — with or without the timestamps, whatever your phone gives you.

How to get the export off your phone

iPhone: open the group → tap the group name at the top → scroll to the bottom → Export Chat → Attach Media → save to Files, or AirDrop it to your laptop.

Android: open the group → ⋮ → More → Export chat → Include media → save to Drive or your computer.

Choose with media. Without it every flyer becomes the words image omitted, and flyers are the most valuable thing in the thread. This page will tell you if that happened.

WhatsApp caps media exports at around 40 MB. On a busy thread that means you'll get recent media and older messages as text only — fine for a weekly habit, worth knowing if you export a year at once.
